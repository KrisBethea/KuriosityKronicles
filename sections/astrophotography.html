Rigel and the Witch Head Nebula

Posted by the Editor on Tuesday November 10, 2026 @5:14PM in Astrophotography
from the cosmic illusion dept.

Reflection nebulae illuminated by bright supergiants always provide stunning visual targets for astrophotographers. IC 2118 near Rigel offers a classic case of pareidolia coupled with intricate interstellar dust structures.

An astrophotograph captures IC 2118, a faint reflection nebula known popularly as the Witch Head Nebula due to its uncanny resemblance to a caricature. Illuminated by the bright blue supergiant Rigel, the sprawling dust cloud spans about 50 light-years.
