Do AI Coding Agents Care About SemVer?

Posted by the Editor on Tuesday November 10, 2026 @5:12PM in AI and Agents
from the semantic versioning dept.

This is a clever piece of practical engineering research that tests an assumption about how autonomous agents interact with codebases. It turns out that version numbers still act as a subtle psychological nudge even for LLMs.

Microsoft researchers tested whether AI coding agents pay attention to semantic versioning when upgrading dependencies. Surprisingly, experiments revealed that version numbers alone changed how models handled byte-identical code modifications.
